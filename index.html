01 Work

Six roles in three years. I chose each one for what it would teach me, not for how it would read.

  1. Washington, DC. I direct a core mission area of Scale’s largest contract, the $151B Golden Dome initiative, developing and deploying cyber effects with a cross-functional team of 50+ engineers.

    I also lead a multi-million-dollar AI security program for an Intelligence Community customer, driving customer syncs, roadmap planning and team coordination, and have authored 10+ strategic reports for IC leadership.

  2. Chantilly, Virginia, in the Cyber Division. I built 153 Splunk panels ingesting over a million network packets, architected AWS VPC Flow Log logging, and presented my findings directly to the Chief AI Officer.

    I also wrote 10+ Python and Scapy traffic generators to automate operations and run hands-on technical training for FBI personnel.

  3. Washington, DC. I deployed five air-gapped AI security environments for defense and Intelligence Community customers on AWS CloudFormation — model scanning, vulnerability detection and runtime threat protection.

    I drove federal sales and capture through demos, RFIs and solution architecture, contributing to the company’s selection for the MDA’s $151B SHIELD IDIQ during a year of 10x ARR growth. I left with an L6 return offer.

  4. Reston, Virginia, on a DoW-funded R&D team. I led a Python agentic pipeline with GitHub and GitLab integrations and air-gapped LLMs for automated code flaw detection, and presented the architecture to GitHub’s CISO and 200+ defense stakeholders.

  5. Arlington, Virginia, in Professional Services. I engineered a full-stack RAG-based AI tool in Python and AWS CDK across 40+ cloud services including Bedrock, S3 and OpenSearch. It shipped in half the projected timeline at under 30ms latency for 100+ concurrent users, tripled the project’s KPIs and expanded its scope by more than 200%.

    I also hosted a company-wide generative AI workshop for 50+ attendees on language models, NLP, vector embeddings and prompt engineering.

  6. Herndon, Virginia. My first job, and I was the only freshman on the team. I built a Python and Google Cloud inventory tracking application for the Army under the $850M ALECS program, cutting latency by 45% and improving performance by 25%, and presented the final build to the CEO.

    I ran identity and access management and Active Directory for 10,000+ users alongside the CIO, started the company’s first campus outreach program, ranked top performer for ticket closures in Q1 2025, and was promoted twice in under a year.

Along the way: network and data engineering for Rockingham County Public Schools, threat intelligence for the Cyber Security Forum Initiative, engineering at Solvitur Systems, and Deloitte’s DLAMP program.
